On-Device AI Flow
01

Capture & Pre-process

Field sentinel captures geo-tagged image via PWA camera API. Image resized to 224×224, normalized for model input. GPS coordinates and timestamp embedded via EXIF.

HTML5 Camera API · IndexedDB
02

MobileNetV3 Inference

Quantized INT8 MobileNetV3 model runs entirely on-device via TensorFlow.js + WASM backend. No network required. Classifies crack type, width estimate, and severity. Typical inference: ~140ms on mid-range device.

TensorFlow.js · WASM Backend · MobileNetV3-INT8
03

Local Triage & Queue

Reports below confidence threshold (0.45) are flagged for human review and stored in IndexedDB. High-confidence reports (≥0.70) are queued for background sync. Low-signal reports are discarded to prevent noise in the risk model.

IndexedDB · Service Worker · Background Sync API
04

Sync & Bayesian Update

When connectivity returns, Workbox background sync pushes the queue to FastAPI backend. Validated reports trigger a Bayesian weight update in the Geo-Evidence Fusion engine, elevating affected Khasra risk scores.

Google Workbox · FastAPI · Geo-Evidence Fusion
